## France: The Eiffel Tower Under Thermal Siege

France is experiencing some of the most intense heatwave conditions in Western Europe, with **large-scale red alerts blanket-covering major tourist regions including Paris and the southern Riviera**.

## Spain: Where Beaches Win, Inland Loses

Spain is facing **extreme summer heat conditions pushing beyond safe tourism thresholds**, especially across southern and central regions where temperatures are climbing into dangerous territory.
