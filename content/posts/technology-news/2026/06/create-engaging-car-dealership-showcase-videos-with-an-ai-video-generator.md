## Rev Up and Dream On with Dreamina!

### Step 1: Type in the prompt and upload an image
Visit Dreamina, log in, then go to the \"AI Video\" section. Upload your photo using the \"Add reference image\", and write a prompt to describe your video. If you are creating a text-to-video, simply type the prompt and do not take a picture.

**Sample prompt:** *\"Generate a polished dealership promotional video of a metallic black SUV spinning under showroom lighting, close-ups of the grille and wheels, seamless camera movements, opulent setting, cinematic reflections, and assertive driving scenes set in a contemporary city.\"*

### Step 2: Generate your video
Once you have added your prompt, select a model (**Seedance 2.0**) to generate your video. Then, select video length and finally an appropriate aspect ratio, 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for TikTok. Lastly, click Dreamina's icon and wait for a couple of seconds to process it.

### Step 3: Enhance & export the video
Polish your video before saving it with Dreamina's AI editing tools. Take advantage of advanced AI features such as **Upscale** for increased resolution, or **Generate soundtrack** for audio. Last but not least, export and share on social media sites.
