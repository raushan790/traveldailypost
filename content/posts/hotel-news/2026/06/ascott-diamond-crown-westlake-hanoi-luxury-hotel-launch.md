## A Landmark Partnership Reshapes Hanoi's Luxury Landscape

**The Ascott Limited**, a wholly-owned subsidiary of **CapitaLand Investment**, has just announced a transformative collaboration with **DOJI Group**, Vietnam's prominent real estate and hospitality conglomerate. Together, they're launching **Diamond Crown Westlake by The Crest Collection**—a 178-key luxury property that signals a seismic shift in how Southeast Asia approaches high-end hospitality.

This isn't just another hotel opening. It's a statement about Vietnam's tourism ambitions and Hanoi's rise as a global luxury destination. The partnership marks Ascott's first venture with DOJI Group and brings an entirely new brand experience to Vietnam's capital city.

## The Crest Collection Arrives in Vietnam

**The Crest Collection**, Ascott's heritage-driven luxury brand, is making its Southeast Asian debut in Hanoi after launching globally in Paris in 2016. The brand operates under a singular philosophy: *"A Story Behind Every Door."*

Each property within The Crest Collection meticulously reflects its locale's cultural identity, blending timeless elegance with contemporary luxury. The brand already commands prestigious addresses across **Paris, Singapore, Bangkok, Tokyo, and London**—now Hanoi joins this exclusive roster.

This expansion underscores Ascott's commitment to delivering culturally immersive experiences rather than standardized hospitality. With Diamond Crown Westlake, Ascott's Vietnam portfolio reaches **40 properties totaling over 10,200 units** across operational and pipeline developments—a staggering footprint across the region.

## Location, Location, Location: Tay Ho's Premier Address

The property occupies a prime lakeside position at **86 Quang An Street** in **Tay Ho**, Hanoi's most exclusive residential enclave. This isn't accidental placement.

Tay Ho is where Hanoi's expatriate community, diplomatic corps, and affluent locals converge. The Quang An Peninsula provides direct access to boutique cafes, international dining venues, lifestyle retail, and—crucially—Hanoi's embassy district. For business travelers, diplomats, and premium leisure guests, the location is magnetic.

The lakeside setting transforms what could be just another urban property into a destination unto itself. You're not simply staying near Hanoi's commercial districts; you're nestled within one of the city's most vibrant, culturally dynamic neighborhoods.

## Inside Diamond Crown Westlake: The Luxury Blueprint

The property features **178 keys** across one- to four-bedroom residences, suites, and duplex units—each designed for extended stays and experiential immersion. This isn't transient hotel architecture; it's residential luxury.

Guest amenities include:
- All-day dining restaurant and café bar
- Executive lounge and reading room
- Full-service spa and fitness center
- Swimming pool and kids' club
- Event and meeting facilities

The design philosophy marries contemporary luxury with authentic Hanoi character. Every detail—from architectural elements to interior curation—reflects local heritage. DOJI Group's involvement ensures meticulous attention to craftsmanship, leveraging their expertise from Vietnam's gold, silver, and gemstone industries.

## Economic Impact: Beyond Hotel Beds

The launch creates immediate employment across hospitality operations, food and beverage services, wellness, and event management. But the economic multiplier extends far deeper.

Local businesses—retail vendors, leisure service providers, transportation operators—will experience increased demand. Cultural tourism activities will flourish. The property becomes a magnet drawing high-value international visitors who spend significantly beyond room rates.

**David Cumming**, Regional General Manager of Indochina at Ascott, stated that Diamond Crown Westlake reflects surging demand for culturally immersive luxury. The Tay Ho neighborhood's international reputation aligns perfectly with The Crest Collection's DNA, positioning the property as both accommodation and cultural destination.

## Vietnam's Tourism Trajectory Shifts Upward

This development arrives amid Vietnam's strategic push to capture premium travel segments. By hosting world-class luxury accommodations, Hanoi strengthens its capacity to attract diplomatic delegations, international conferences, and high-spending leisure travelers.

The property signals to global luxury brands that Vietnam offers viable, competitive markets for upscale hospitality. Repeat visitation increases when travelers discover authentic cultural experiences paired with refined amenities. Longer stays generate higher per-room spending. Destination differentiation—setting Hanoi apart from competing Southeast Asian cities—becomes reality.

**Ms. Le Thi Hien** of DOJI Group emphasized the partnership's ambition: elevating Vietnam's tourism sector globally through premium accommodations and resort experiences appealing to both international and domestic markets.

## A Broader Hospitality Trend

Diamond Crown Westlake reflects a global luxury hospitality trend where properties serve as cultural storytellers, not mere sleep destinations. Travelers increasingly seek authentic connections to place—architecture reflecting heritage, cuisine rooted in local traditions, experiences enabling genuine community interaction.

This approach drives occupancy rates, guest satisfaction, and sustainable tourism growth. When travelers feel they've experienced a destination's authentic soul rather than a generic luxury environment, they become advocates. They return. They recommend.

The property also supports ancillary industries: local food producers, artisanal retail, wellness practitioners, cultural activity providers. This creates a sustainable economic ecosystem where tourism benefits ripple through entire communities.

## Hanoi's Position as Southeast Asia's Luxury Capital

Diamond Crown Westlake sets a new benchmark for design-led, culturally integrated hospitality in Hanoi. It positions the city competitively against Bangkok, Singapore, and other regional luxury destinations. It signals Vietnam's investment in infrastructure, hospitality standards, and visitor experiences.

For nomadic professionals seeking extended stays in premium environments, properties like Diamond Crown Westlake offer something crucial: connection to place. You're not isolated in a corporate tower; you're embedded in Hanoi's living culture, with access to global-standard amenities and personalized service.

The development aligns with Vietnam's broader tourism strategy of attracting affluent travelers, international business guests, and relocating professionals—demographics that generate sustained economic activity and drive destination development.
