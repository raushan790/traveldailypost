The strikes sent shockwaves through Kuwait's petrochemical infrastructure and Bahrain's strategic military installations. Shipping companies are increasingly rerouting vessels through longer, more expensive passages—adding weeks to transit times and inflating fuel costs. Insurance premiums for vessels transiting the Gulf have spiked, with some insurers charging premiums triple those from just two weeks prior.

This disruption has immediate ripple effects on global energy prices. Oil futures markets are volatile. Natural gas shipments are delayed. And the longer-term supply chain implications could reverberate through industries worldwide.
