## Bora Bora's Luxury Culinary Crown Gets a New Master

The tropical paradise of **French Polynesia** just got significantly more delicious. **Four Seasons Resort Bora Bora**, already distinguished with two **Michelin Keys**, has announced a game-changing appointment that signals serious intent to dominate the ultra-luxury dining scene across Oceania. The resort has tapped **Executive Chef Mickael Messina**—a 25-year international culinary veteran—to orchestrate its entire gastronomic empire.

This isn't just another chef hire. This is a strategic power move in the hyper-competitive world of resort hospitality, where dining experiences have become as critical as oceanfront villas and spa treatments. With Messina now commanding the culinary direction, the Four Seasons Bora Bora is positioning itself to set new benchmarks for what luxury island dining can achieve.

## The Chef Behind the Headlines

Who exactly is **Mickael Messina**, and why should luxury travelers care? His resume reads like a master class in global culinary excellence.

Messina cut his teeth in France, completing his foundational training at the **Centre de Formation à Villepinte (CEFAA)** in Villepinte and the **Institut National de Formation et d'Application Le Manoir** in Gouvieux—institutions known for producing culinary professionals, not amateurs. His career formally launched in 2000 across northern France, where he built mastery in professional kitchen environments.

The turning point came in **2004**, when **Messina accepted a Junior Sous Chef position at The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman**—his first taste of island resort hospitality. This experience proved transformative, establishing his credentials in the exact hospitality ecosystem where he now operates.

## A Culinary Journey Across Five Continents

What separates Messina from run-of-the-mill resort chefs is his refusal to stay in one place. His trajectory has been deliberately cosmopolitan—a strategy that builds the kind of creative flexibility and cultural awareness that defines world-class culinary leadership.

**2006–2015: The Paris Years**

After his Caribbean stint, Messina returned to France and worked across some of Paris's most prestigious establishments. His portfolio included roles at **Angelina** (the legendary tearoom destination), **La Maison** in Senlis, **Open House** in Apremont, and the iconic **Hôtel Plaza Athénée**—collectively representing the gold standard of French gastronomy. These weren't just jobs; they were masterclasses in refined service and ingredient precision.

**2016–2024: The Asian Expansion**

In 2016, Messina pivoted toward Asia, taking on **Executive Chef responsibilities in Hong Kong** across three distinct properties: **Stan Cafe**, **Wooloomooloo Steakhouse**, and **Bacchus Wine & Restaurant**. This diversified portfolio—spanning cafe culture, steakhouse precision, and wine gastronomy—demonstrates his ability to operate across different culinary genres with equal authority.

His most recent position, before the Four Seasons call, was a **two-year tenure as Executive Chef at Cheval Blanc Randheli in the Maldives**. That ultra-luxury Maldivian resort appointment is particularly significant: it confirms that Messina operates comfortably at the absolute apex of resort hospitality, in an environment where guests expect flawless execution and innovation in equal measure.

## The Scope of Messina's New Command

At the Four Seasons Bora Bora, Messina takes control of an expansive culinary operation that extends far beyond a single flagship restaurant. His remit encompasses oversight of **four distinct restaurant properties, in-room dining services, banquets, and event dining operations**—essentially, he controls every plate that leaves a kitchen at the resort.

This architectural responsibility means Messina must deliver consistency across multiple dining formats while maintaining distinct identities for each venue. It's a complexity that separates elite chefs from generalists.

## Why This Appointment Matters Now

**Romain Chanet**, General Manager of Four Seasons Resort Bora Bora, positioned this move deliberately: the appointment arrives precisely as the resort is capitalizing on its recent **Michelin Key recognition**. That accolade raised the bar. Now, Messina's leadership must ensure that the culinary programme doesn't just meet that standard—it expands and refines it.
