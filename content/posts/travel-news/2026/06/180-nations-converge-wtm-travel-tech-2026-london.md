## The Global Stage for Travel Technology Innovation

November 2026 is about to transform London into the epicenter of global travel innovation. Over **180 nations**—from China and the United States to Brazil, Mexico, Switzerland, Argentina, Qatar, Germany, Jamaica, Italy, and Spain—will converge at **WTM Travel Tech 2026** to unveil groundbreaking digital platforms that are reshaping how we explore the world.

This isn't just another trade show. With an expected attendance of **46,500 travel and tech professionals**, the three-day event promises to facilitate over **£2.8 billion in international business deals**, cementing its position as the premier global stage for travel technology leadership.

## A Historic Gathering of Global Leaders

The scale of international participation speaks volumes about the travel industry's urgent pivot toward technology-driven solutions. **ExCeL London** will host delegates and exhibitors representing every inhabited continent, creating unprecedented opportunities for cross-border partnerships and innovation exchange.

The geographic breadth is staggering. Africa and the Middle East will send representatives from 29 countries, including South Africa, Egypt, and the United Arab Emirates. ## The Innovation Highway: Where the Future Takes Shape

At the beating heart of WTM Travel Tech sits the **Innovation Highway**—a sprawling exhibition space where the travel industry's most disruptive technologies collide with real-world applications.

Expect to see groundbreaking demonstrations across artificial intelligence, machine learning, Internet of Things sensors, predictive analytics, cloud infrastructure, mobile applications, and immersive technologies.
