**Disney Springs is about to explode with a gaming revolution.**

On **June 29, 2026**, a massive interactive entertainment venue called **Level99** will transform the former NBA Experience space on Disney Springs' West Side into something we haven't seen before in Central Florida — a 46,800 square-foot, two-floor real-world gaming playground with over 60 life-sized challenge rooms. This isn't just another arcade. This is a full-blown experiential destination designed to keep visitors at Disney Springs longer, spending more, and coming back repeatedly.

I've been tracking the Orlando tourism scene for years, and this opening signals something critical: Disney Springs is no longer positioning itself as a shopping district. It's becoming a full-day leisure ecosystem that rivals theme parks in terms of immersion and engagement.

## What Exactly Is Level99?

Level99 operates on a straightforward business model: pay once, play everything. Ticket pricing starts at **$29.99 per player**, with timed sessions running **1.5 to 2.5 hours**. During that time, guests access all challenges without additional per-game fees — a refreshing departure from the traditional arcade model.

The venue is engineered around three core game formats:

- **Cooperative challenge rooms** requiring teams to solve puzzles together
- **Competitive duels** pitting players or teams against each other
- **Interactive scavenger hunts** that blend movement, logic, and exploration

Each individual challenge typically lasts one to four minutes, making the experience fast-paced and variety-driven. Games are designed for teams of two to six players, making it ideal for families, friend groups, and corporate team-building events.

## The Technology Layer: RFID Velobands

Here's where Level99 gets interesting beyond the physical gameplay.

Every guest receives an **RFID-enabled Veloband** upon entry — essentially a smart wristband that tracks progress, unlocks challenges in sequence, and stores player performance data across multiple visits. This gamification layer creates a powerful repeat-visit incentive. Players will naturally want to return to improve their scores, compete with previous records, and climb leaderboards.

This is modern tourism psychology at work. The venue isn't designed for one-and-done visits. It's architected for loyalty.

## Game Variety That Keeps You Coming Back

Level99 features signature challenge concepts that span difficulty levels and physical requirements:

- **Axe-running obstacle lanes** for action-oriented players
- **Rope navigation puzzles** combining strength and coordination
- **Logic-based banquet seating challenges** for tactical thinkers
- Multiple non-physical puzzle rooms for guests with mobility considerations

This deliberate variety ensures both competitive athletes and casual players find engaging content. No one's left standing idle. No one feels excluded.

## The Hospitality Integration: Dining & Drinking

This is critical to understanding Level99's tourism strategy. The venue isn't gaming-only. It's a full hospitality play.

A **two-story bar** serves craft cocktails, craft beers, and locally-inspired drinks. Food programming includes **Detroit-style pizza**, **wagyu beef burgers**, and shareable snacks prepared on-site. This combination — gaming + dining + drinks — transforms Level99 from an activity into a destination.

Disney Springs West Side already hosts heavy-hitting entertainment anchors like **Cirque du Soleil's "Drawn to Life"** theatre, **Splitsville Luxury Lanes**, and **AMC Disney Springs 24**. Level99 fits seamlessly into this entertainment cluster, creating a genuine alternative to theme park days.

## Why Location Matters: Disney Springs Context

Let me be clear about geography here. **Disney Springs is not a theme park.** It's a free-entry retail and entertainment district within Walt Disney World Resort. The district is divided into four zones: **Marketplace**, **The Landing**, **Town Center**, and **West Side**.

The West Side specifically is known for nightlife, live shows, and entertainment venues — exactly where Level99 belongs. According to [official Walt Disney World data](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining-entertainment/disneys-springs/), Disney Springs already attracts millions of annual visitors due to free entry, parking access, and proximity to Orlando's broader tourism infrastructure.

Level99 continues a deliberate strategic shift toward experience-driven entertainment that extends visitor dwell time at Walt Disney World property — and the resort's bottom line.

Level99 isn't gatekeeping fun. The venue includes **accessibility accommodations** with varied difficulty levels and non-physical puzzle rooms for guests with mobility limitations. The progression-based leaderboard system rewards returning guests, creating a built-in loyalty mechanism.
