## The Global Credentials Behind the Leadership

Bonthuis brings something rare to the table: he's worked at sea, in island paradises, and in some of the world's most demanding luxury hotel markets. His journey started aboard a major cruise line, where he navigated operational roles across **Hawaii, French Polynesia, New Zealand, and Indonesia**—exposure that shaped his understanding of diverse international guest expectations from day one.

The real pivot came in 2014 when he transitioned to land-based luxury hospitality. His career progressed through **Curaçao and Macau** before he landed at **Four Seasons Hotel Macao**, where he climbed from Restaurant Manager to Director of Food and Beverage. That's not a lateral move—that's proof he can execute at the highest operational levels.

At Macao, Bonthuis didn't just manage existing dining concepts. He shaped them. He was hands-on during the pre-opening of The Grand Suites, an all-suite luxury property, and developed multiple dining concepts including a **Michelin-starred Chinese restaurant** and a contemporary ramen outlet. This is someone who understands that modern luxury dining requires both culinary excellence and operational precision.
