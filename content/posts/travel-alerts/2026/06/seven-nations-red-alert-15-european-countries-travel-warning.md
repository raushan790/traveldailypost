Officials framed the move as a **defensive preventive measure**, not a punitive sanction. ## Belarus and Ukraine: The Active War Zones

**Belarus** was placed under the harshest advisory after diplomats confirmed that military units had been mobilized and that missile launch platforms were positioned on Belarusian soil. The **United Kingdom's travel guidance** explicitly stated that *all* travel to Belarus must be avoided and that consular support could **not be guaranteed**. Officials warned that foreign nationals faced arbitrary detention and that the neighboring Ukrainian conflict could spill across the border without warning.

In **Ukraine**, the situation was described as "dire." Drone and missile attacks had intensified dramatically. The government urged residents and visitors to shelter immediately when air raid alerts sounded. Official restrictions designated:

- All areas within 50 kilometers of the Belarus border as off-limits
- Occupied territories of **Crimea, Donetsk, and Luhansk** as completely inaccessible
- Major cities facing infrastructure destruction from sustained attacks

Humanitarian corridors were being monitored, but cross-border artillery fire remained constant. The red alerts issued by the allied seven were justified by official casualty data and intelligence reports documenting rapid escalation of hostilities.

## Russia: Detention Risks and Terrorist Threats

**Russia** received one of the most stringent Do Not Travel advisories ever issued. Official briefings documented cases of foreign nationals detained and used as leverage in diplomatic disputes. Drone attacks on major cities had heightened the general **terrorism threat level**. The suspension of many international flights left travelers with virtually no departure options.

The warnings stated plainly: **consular assistance might be impossible**. Terrorist attacks were described as likely, and restrictions on foreign media meant that travelers would have limited information access during emergencies. These factors combined to create an enforcement of the red alert.

## The Domino Effect: Moldova, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia

The breakaway Moldovan region of **Transnistria** was flagged as a zone where all travel must be avoided. Russian forces and local militias control access, and administrative boundary lines are heavily fortified. Visitors crossing these lines face detention, interrogation, or deportation. Villages near boundaries are reported depopulated, and sporatic skirmishes continue to be documented.
