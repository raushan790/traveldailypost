## The Strait of Hormuz: Why This Chokepoint Dominates Every Travel Decision

One maritime corridor explains everything happening right now: the **Strait of Hormuz**. This narrow passage between Iran and Oman controls roughly **30% of global liquefied natural gas** and a staggering share of international oil shipments. It's not hyperbole to say that disruption here reshapes energy markets worldwide.

For travelers, airlines, and maritime operators, the Strait represents a single point of failure. When tensions rise, shipping insurance premiums spike. Airspace closures force costly rerouting. Even minor incidents trigger cascading operational complications across every major airline network serving the region.

Recent diplomatic signals have temporarily eased concerns about sudden chokepoint disruption. But security agencies continue intensive surveillance. The fragility of the current calm means that even limited escalation could reverse all recent advisory improvements within hours.
