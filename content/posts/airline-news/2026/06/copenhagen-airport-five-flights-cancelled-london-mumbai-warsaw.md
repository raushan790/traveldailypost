## What Passengers Can Expect

Affected travelers should understand their rights under **EU261/2004 regulation**, which mandates compensation for cancellations under specific circumstances. Airlines must offer either rebooking on alternative flights or full refunds plus compensation up to **€600** depending on distance and circumstances.

The real challenge? Processing these claims during peak summer travel season means delays in actual compensation delivery. Many passengers will eventually receive compensation, but not before enduring significant travel disruption and financial inconvenience.

## The Broader Travel Lesson
