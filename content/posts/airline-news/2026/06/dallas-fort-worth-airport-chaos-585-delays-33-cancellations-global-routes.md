## Why Competitors Largely Escaped Unscathed

Here's where the crisis reveals a critical distinction in operational strategy. Unlike **American Airlines**, which bases crew and aircraft at DFW as its primary hub, carriers like **Delta Air Lines** and **United Airlines** operate DFW as mere "outstations." They fly aircraft in from other hubs, cycle them back out, and leave before the rolling crew and gate backlogs can trap them.

The result? Competitors remained largely insulated from the carnage:

- **Delta Air Lines**: Just 1 cancellation and 3 delays (4% delay rate)
- **United Airlines**: 16 delays, zero cancellations (0% cancellation rate)
- **SkyWest Airlines**: 32 delays, zero cancellations
- **Frontier Airlines**: 19 delays, zero cancellations

Other carriers including **Air Canada** (3 delays), **Sun Country Airlines** (5 delays), and **Alaska Airlines** (1 delay) navigated the crisis with minimal damage. The crisis was, unmistakably, an American Airlines operational emergency.

## What Stranded Passengers Should Do Right Now

With airlines choosing to delay rather than cancel, tens of thousands of travelers face prolonged terminal waits. Here's what you need to do immediately:

### Bypass the Terminal Counter Madness

Airport customer service lines are overwhelmed. Instead, **download the American Airlines mobile app immediately** and search for automatic rebooking options or alternate flight paths. If the app is slow, contact the airline's international customer service line — Canadian or UK numbers often connect to agents with shorter queues who have identical rebooking authority.

### Document Everything for Compensation Claims

Under **EU Regulation 261/2004** and similar rules across jurisdictions, you may be entitled to compensation for delays exceeding three hours. Take photos of departure boards, keep your boarding pass, and document the actual departure time. File a claim with [AirHelp](https://www.airhelp.com) or similar services once you've landed safely.

### Consider Paid Rebooking on Competitors

If your American Airlines flight won't depart until evening or next morning, **Delta, United, or Southwest** may have available seats on similar routes. Some airlines will book you on competitors during operational disruptions — always ask before accepting the rebooking.

### Stay Hydrated and Document Expenses

Airlines must provide meals, refreshments, and accommodation if the delay stretches beyond your control. Keep all receipts. You may be reimbursed up to approximately $100-$300 depending on flight length and jurisdiction.
