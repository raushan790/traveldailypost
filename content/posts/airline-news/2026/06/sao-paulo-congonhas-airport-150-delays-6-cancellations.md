## What Went Wrong? The Perfect Storm of Delays

Airport experts rarely point to a single culprit when massive delays hit. Instead, disruptions typically result from a combustible mix of operational pressures converging at the worst moment.

**Weather conditions** top the list. Heavy rain, reduced visibility, or strong winds can ground aircraft or slow ground operations to a crawl. **Air traffic congestion** amplifies the problem—Congonhas is one of Brazil's busiest airports, and peak travel periods create bottlenecks that affect every subsequent departure.

Then there's **aircraft rotation**. Modern airlines squeeze maximum utilization from their fleets, with domestic aircraft operating five, six, sometimes seven flights per day. One early delay cascades through the entire schedule like dominoes.

**Ground operations**—passenger boarding, baggage handling, maintenance checks, fueling—each add minutes to turnaround times. Add these together during peak hours, and a 10-minute delay becomes 30 becomes 90.

Finally, **Brazil's air navigation systems** coordinate thousands of daily flights. Any adjustment to traffic flow from the country's air traffic management authority ripples through airport schedules instantly.

## What Rights Do Stranded Passengers Actually Have?

Brazil's **National Civil Aviation Agency (ANAC)** establishes clear passenger protection standards. [ANAC's official guidelines](https://www.anac.gov.br/) outline what airlines must provide during disruptions.

For delays exceeding certain thresholds:

**Communication Support** — Airlines must provide timely, accurate information about delays and schedule changes. No more cryptic gate changes with zero explanation.

**Food and Refreshments** — Extended delays trigger meal vouchers or direct food service, depending on airline policies and delay duration.

**Accommodation** — Overnight delays require hotels and ground transportation. Airlines can't simply strand passengers.

**Rebooking Rights** — Cancelled flights mean either rebooking on the next available flight or full refunds, with compensation in certain scenarios.

The reality? Many passengers don't know these rights exist. Airlines bank on this ignorance. Affected travelers should contact **ANAC directly** if carriers refuse assistance.
