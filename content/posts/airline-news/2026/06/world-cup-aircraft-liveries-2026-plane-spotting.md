## Terminal Observation: Your Spotting Strategy

Planning to catch these aircraft in person? The logistics matter.

**First: Identify your vantage points.** Major host gateways like **Los Angeles International (LAX)**, **Miami International (MIA)**, and **Dallas/Fort Worth (DFW)** offer public observation decks with unobstructed runway views. These locations provide optimal daylight angles for photography without requiring security clearance.

**Second: Monitor real-time tracking.** Specialized aviation tracking platforms and forums publish tail numbers and scheduled movements. By tracking specific aircraft registrations rather than generic flight paths, you can predict arrival times down to the minute. **Third: Keep your camera ready during layovers.** These custom widebodies regularly park at standard international gates. Lucky passengers get up-close views of intricate details—the graphics quality, paint schemes, and team insignia that photographs don't fully capture from a distance.
