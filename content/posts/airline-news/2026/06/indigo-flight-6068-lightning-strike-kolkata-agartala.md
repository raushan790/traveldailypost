During severe thunderstorm penetration, that frequency increases significantly.

Yet modern aviation's safety record with lightning strikes is exceptional. Aircraft are built to handle these events through:

- **Conductive aluminum fuselages** that safely distribute electrical charge
- **Peripheral bonding systems** that protect avionics from surge damage

- **Advanced materials** in engines and composite structures

The aircraft's protection systems did exactly what they were designed to do: channel the lethal electrical discharge safely away from critical systems and passengers.
