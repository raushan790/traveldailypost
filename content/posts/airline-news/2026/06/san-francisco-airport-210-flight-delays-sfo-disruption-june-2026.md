## What You Should Do If Caught in SFO Chaos

If your flight gets caught in a disruption like this, follow these critical steps:

**Stay informed in real time.** Don't rely on in-flight announcements alone. Check your airline's app, [FlightAware](https://flightaware.com), or directly contact your carrier's customer service desk. Real-time data beats delayed announcements every time.

**Know your rebooking rights.** Different airlines apply different policies. Some automatically rebook you on the next available flight. Others require you to request it. Ask immediately—don't assume.

**Understand compensation eligibility.** US Department of Transportation regulations mandate compensation for certain delays and cancellations, but the specifics depend on flight distance and cause. Ask your airline about your rights under [DOT regulations](https://www.transportation.gov/airconsumer).

**Plan ahead for future SFO flights.** Build longer layovers when connecting through SFO. Two-hour connections are risky. Three or four hours provides buffer. Monitor flight status the day before travel and the morning of departure.

**Be flexible.** If your flight is cancelled, immediately ask about alternative routings—even if they require circuitous paths through other hubs. Getting there late beats not getting there at all.
