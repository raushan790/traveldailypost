Here's what's remarkable: **Etihad is currently operating at 85 percent of total capacity**, and they're not waiting for full recovery to expand. By mid-June 2026, overall capacity was set to increase by **10 percent** compared to the previous year, with bookings projected to reach pre-conflict levels by August.

This isn't reckless optimism. The data backs it up.

While the aviation industry collectively faces an estimated **$100 billion in additional fuel costs during 2026**, Etihad's hedging program has effectively cushioned the carrier from fuel price spikes.
